2. Grammar: Omitting the Agent

When explaining a process (how things are made, grown, or recycled), we usually omit the agent (the person doing the action) because it is obvious or unimportant.

Adding the Agent
We use "by" if the person is specific and important.
"The HUB is designed by Teacher Ivan."
Omitting the Agent
Do not say "by people" or "by workers". Just cut it!
"The paper is collected every Friday." (We don't need to say 'by the garbage men').
🚨 THE "SE" TRANSLATION TRAP

In Spanish, we use the pronoun "se" for passive processes: "Aquí se recicla papel". Do NOT translate this as "Paper recycles here" (El papel no se recicla a sí mismo). You must use the Passive Voice.

❌ Paper recycles here.
✅ Paper IS RECYCLED here.

Reading Context: The Green Campaign

"At Colegio Fielding, we are promoting a new green campaign. Every week, tons of paper are used by the students in their exams and homework. Now, instead of throwing it away, this paper is collected every Friday."

"After that, the materials are sorted into different bins: plastic, glass, and paper. Finally, the paper is taken to a special facility in Sucre where it is recycled and reused for future notebooks."

"At home, Lau and I also try to recycle. However, our cardboard boxes are completely destroyed by Perlita before we can recycle them! The boxes are used as her personal scratching posts. I guess that is also a form of 'reusing'!"
