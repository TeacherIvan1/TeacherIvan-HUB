Unless and Provided that

Objective: Set strict rules and conditions using formal conditional connectors.

1. Vocabulary: Model United Nations (MUN)

Learn these words to speak formally in a debate or academic environment.

👔 Delegate Delegado/a (Representante)
🗣️ Debate Debatir / El debate
📚 Research Investigar / Investigación
✅ Attend Asistir (a un evento o clase)

2. Grammar: Formal Conditionals

When you are in a formal situation (like a Model United Nations), saying "If" or "As long as" can sound too casual. Let's upgrade our conditionals.

Provided that
Provided that + Condition, Result

It means exactly the same as "As long as" or "Only if", but it is much more formal. It sets a strict rule.

"Provided that you study, you will pass." (Con la condición de que estudies...)
Unless (Review)
Unless + Positive Verb, Result

Remember: it means "If not". Never put "don't" or "doesn't" after it!

"Unless you attend the meeting, you won't participate." (A menos que asistas...)
🚨 THE "ATTEND" vs "ASSIST" TRAP

This is a legendary False Friend! In Spanish, "Asistir" means going to a place. In English, ASSIST means "Ayudar" (Help). If you want to say "Yo asisto a la escuela", you MUST say ATTEND.

❌ I assist to Colegio Fielding every day.
✅ I ATTEND Colegio Fielding every day.

Reading Context: The SAGMUN 2026 Challenge

"As the teacher in charge of the Model United Nations (MUN) commission at Colegio Fielding, I have a big project right now. In June, 14 of my students are going to participate in SAGMUN 2026. It is a very prestigious event!"

"Because it is a formal debate, I established strict rules. A student can be a delegate provided that they attend all the preparation meetings on Thursdays. Unless they research the history of their assigned countries, they won't know how to defend their arguments!"

"My students are brilliant, and I know they will win awards provided that they speak confidently in English. Meanwhile, Perlita the cat thinks she is a delegate too. But unless she learns how to speak human languages, she will have to stay home in Sucre!"
