2. Grammar: Active vs. Passive

Usually, we focus on WHO does the action (Active Voice). But sometimes, the action itself is more important, or we don't know who did it. Then, we use the Passive Voice.

Active Voice
Subject + Verb + Object

Focus on the person.

"The janitor cleans the classroom every day."
Passive Voice
Object + AM/IS/ARE + V3 (Past Participle)

Focus on the object.

"The classroom is cleaned every day (by the janitor)."
🚨 THE FORGOTTEN "TO BE" TRAP

In Spanish, we sometimes use "se" to make a passive sentence ("Se limpia el cuarto"). Because of this, students forget the verb "To be" in English. You MUST use am, is, or are before the verb!

❌ The desks arranged every afternoon.
✅ The desks ARE arranged every afternoon.
