Opinion Essays

Objective: Learn how to structure a strong argument and avoid neutrality in "Agree or Disagree" prompts.

1. Vocabulary: Expressing Strong Stances

Upgrade your vocabulary from basic B1 to Band 7+ academic English.

🗣️ Assert Afirmar con convicción Instead of: Say
🧠 Firmly believe Creer firmemente Instead of: I think
📈 Beneficial Beneficioso / Positivo Instead of: Good
📉 Detrimental Perjudicial / Dañino Instead of: Bad

2. Strategy: The Clear Thesis

The most common prompt in Task 2 is: "To what extent do you agree or disagree?". Your examiner is looking for one thing in your introduction: a crystal-clear position.

The Thesis Statement
This is the last sentence of your introduction. It tells the examiner exactly what you will argue.
I completely agree/disagree that...

"I firmly believe that technology is beneficial for education."

The Body Paragraphs
If you agree, BOTH of your body paragraphs must support why you agree. Do not waste a paragraph discussing the opposite side.
Body 1: Reason 1 + Example
Body 2: Reason 2 + Example
🚨 THE FENCE-SITTING TRAP

"Sitting on the fence" means refusing to take a side. In Spanish academic writing, it is common to say "Tiene cosas buenas y cosas malas". DO NOT do this in an IELTS Opinion Essay. If you say "I agree a little bit, but I also disagree", you will lose massive points in the Task Response category.

❌ This topic has advantages and disadvantages.
✅ I completely agree with this statement because...

Reading Context: The Physical Education Debate

Prompt: "Some people think that physical education is as important as academic subjects. To what extent do you agree or disagree?"

"As an educator at Colegio Fielding, I have a very clear stance on this. In my essay introduction, I would write: 'I firmly believe that physical training is just as crucial as academic study for a student’s overall development.'"

"For my first body paragraph, I would argue that morning exercise improves mental focus. I would use my own 5:30 AM running routine in Sucre as a real-world example of how discipline builds concentration. For my second paragraph, I would assert that team sports teach resilience. My intense CrossFit sessions with Lau prove that physical challenges are highly beneficial for overcoming stress."

"In my conclusion, I would state that while studying Philosophy and History is vital, a purely sedentary lifestyle is detrimental to human health. Unless my students want to end up like Perlita the cat, sleeping passively all day, physical activity must remain a mandatory pillar in our schools!"
