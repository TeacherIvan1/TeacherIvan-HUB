Project: A Short Story

Objective: Write a 100-word narrative using Past Tenses and Advanced Connectors.

1. Your Writing Mission 🚀

In international B1 exams, you must be able to tell a compelling story. It is time to act like a movie director and use the three "narrative cameras" we practiced.

✍️ The Prompt

Write a short story about an unexpected event or a difficult trip. You must include:

  • Past Continuous: To set the atmosphere or describe a long action (While I was...).
  • Past Simple: To describe the main actions.
  • Past Perfect: To explain something that happened *before* the main story (I had forgotten...).
  • Connectors: Use at least two (Meanwhile, As soon as, Until, Eventually, So, Because).

Write at least 100 words.

Teacher Ivan's Example: The Santa Cruz Incident

"Last year, my girlfriend and I set off for a CrossFit competition in Santa Cruz. While we were travelling on the bus, it started to rain heavily. Suddenly, the engine made a terrible noise and the bus broke down! We were completely held up.

I was very stressed because my phone battery died, and I had forgotten to charge it the night before. I couldn't check my 'Teacher Ivan - Lifestyle' app! Meanwhile, our competitors were already warming up in the stadium.

We waited on the dark road until a mechanic arrived. As soon as he fixed the bus, we continued. Eventually, we arrived in Santa Cruz. We were exhausted, but we had made it just in time!"
