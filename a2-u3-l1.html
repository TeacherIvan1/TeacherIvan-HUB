Academic Goals

Objective: Talk about your future plans and study goals using "Be going to".

1. Vocabulary: Education Goals

Learn these action verbs to describe what you want to achieve in school.

📖 Study Estudiar
✅ Pass (an exam) Aprobar (un examen)
📈 Improve Mejorar
🎓 Graduate Graduarse / Terminar

2. Grammar: Future Plans (Be going to)

When we talk about a plan or a clear intention for the future, we use "be going to". It is very common when talking about our academic goals.

Affirmative (+)
Subject + am/is/are + going to + Verb
"I am going to study English."
"She is going to pass the exam."
"We are going to improve."
Negative (-)
Subject + am/is/are NOT + going to + Verb
"I am not going to fail."
"He isn't going to study today."
"They aren't going to give up."
🚨 THE MISSING "BE" TRAP

Because in Spanish we say "Yo voy a estudiar" (3 words), students often forget the verb "To Be" (am/is/are) in English, or they forget the word "to" before the action verb.

❌ I going to study. (¡Falta el "am"!)
❌ She is going pass. (¡Falta el "to"!)
✅ I AM going TO study.
