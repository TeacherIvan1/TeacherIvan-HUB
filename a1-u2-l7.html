Weekend Plans

Objective: Talk about your plans for the future using "Be going to".

1. Vocabulary: Weekend Activities

What do you do on Saturday and Sunday? Learn these verbs.

🛋️ Relax Relajarse
💻 Write code Escribir código / Programar
🧹 Clean the house Limpiar la casa
🍿 Watch a movie Ver una película

2. Grammar: Be going to

To talk about a plan in the future, we use Be + going to + verb. It means "Voy a...".

Affirmative (+)
Subject + am/is/are + going to + Verb
"I am going to relax today." (Yo voy a relajarme hoy).
"She is going to study." (Ella va a estudiar).
Negative (-)
Subject + am not / isn't / aren't + going to + Verb
"I am not going to work." (Yo no voy a trabajar).
"We aren't going to clean." (Nosotros no vamos a limpiar).
🚨 The Missing "To Be" Trap

Never forget the verb To Be (am / is / are)! If you say "I going to", it is incorrect in English.

❌ I going to watch a movie.
✅ I AM going to watch a movie.

Reading Context: April Plans in Sucre

"It is April 2026. The weekend is here! This weekend, I am not going to teach at Colegio Fielding. I am going to relax with my girlfriend."

"On Saturday morning, we are going to train CrossFit. Then, in the afternoon, I am going to write code in HTML for my app. I have many ideas!"

"Perlita the cat isn't going to write code. She isn't going to clean the house. She is going to sleep on the sofa. That is her plan for the weekend!"
