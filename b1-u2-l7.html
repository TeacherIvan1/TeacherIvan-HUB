Time Connectors

Objective: Link past events smoothly using Until, As soon as, Meanwhile, and Eventually.

1. Vocabulary: Narrative Sequencing

Learn these four connectors to give your stories a professional, fluent rhythm.

⚡ As soon as Tan pronto como (Inmediatamente)
⏳ Until Hasta que
↔️ Meanwhile Mientras tanto (Al mismo tiempo)
🏁 Eventually Finalmente / Al final

2. Grammar: How to use them

These connectors act as the "glue" between your sentences. They tell the listener exactly *when* the next action happened.

As soon as (Immediate)
Action 1 happens, and Action 2 happens zero seconds later.
"As soon as the class finished, the students ran out."
Meanwhile (Parallel)
Action 1 is happening here. At the same time, Action 2 is happening somewhere else.
"I was studying hard. Meanwhile, my cat was sleeping."
🚨 THE "EVENTUALLY" FALSE FRIEND

In Spanish, "eventualmente" means "de vez en cuando" or "posiblemente". This is a FALSE FRIEND. In English, Eventually means "Finally, after a long time or a lot of effort" (Al final / Tarde o temprano).

❌ I eventually eat pizza on weekends. (I sometimes eat pizza).
✅ It rained for three days. Eventually, the sun came out. (Finalmente salió el sol).

Reading Context: The App Launch

"Yesterday was a very important day at Colegio Fielding. It was the official launch of my new application, 'Teacher Ivan - Lifestyle'. The night before, I tested the HTML and CSS code until 3:00 AM to make sure it was perfect."

"In the morning, I presented the app to my students. However, as soon as I clicked the 'publish' button, the school's server crashed! I was incredibly stressed. I worked frantically to fix the server connection. Meanwhile, Perlita was at home, peacefully sleeping on my laptop charger, completely ignoring my panic."

"I tried different codes and restarted the system three times. It was exhausting. But eventually, the server connected. The app went live, and the students successfully downloaded it to track their habits and grades. It was a great victory!"
