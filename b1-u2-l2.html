Interrupted Workouts

Objective: Ask Past Continuous questions and use "Just as" for dramatic interruptions.

1. Vocabulary: Gym Accidents & Actions

To tell a good gym story, you need the right verbs. Let's learn some advanced fitness terms.

🏋️ Lift weights Levantar pesas Past: Lifted
🤸 Do muscle-ups Hacer muscle-ups Past: Did
🖐️ Lose grip Perder el agarre / Resbalarse Past: Lost grip
🔌 Power goes out Se va la luz / Corte eléctrico Past: Power went out

2. Grammar: Questions & "Just as"

When someone tells you a crazy story, you need to know how to ask what was happening at that exact moment. We use the Past Continuous Question structure.

Past Continuous Questions
(Wh-) + was/were + Subject + V-ing?

Move the verb To Be (was/were) before the person.

"What were you doing when the power went out?"
"Was she lifting weights?"
Advanced Connector: Just as
Just as + Past Continuous

Just as means "Justo cuando". It replaces "while" to make the interruption feel more dramatic and immediate.

"Just as I was finishing my exercise, I dropped the bar."
🚨 WHAT DID YOU DO? vs. WHAT WERE YOU DOING?

Be very careful! These two questions mean completely different things in English:

  • What were you doing when he arrived? = What action was already in progress? (I was taking a shower).
  • What did you do when he arrived? = What was your reaction *after* he arrived? (I opened the door).

Reading Context: The CrossFit Incident

"Yesterday afternoon, my girlfriend and I were training at the CrossFit box in Sucre. It was a heavy session. We were practicing our Clean and Jerks when suddenly, the music stopped. The power went out!"

Friend: "Oh no! What were you doing when it happened?"

Ivan: "Just as I was lifting the heavy bar over my head, everything went dark! It was terrifying. But that wasn't the only accident. Later, just as I was doing my final muscle-up on the rings, I lost my grip and fell on the mat!"

Friend: "Ouch! Were you hurting your ankle?"

Ivan: "No, I was fine, just embarrassed. When we got back home, we found Perlita. What was she doing? She was sleeping directly on top of my sweaty gym bag!"
