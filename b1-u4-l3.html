The school was built in...

Objective: Talk about historical facts and past events using the Past Simple Passive.

1. Vocabulary: Historical Verbs

Learn these irregular and regular verbs to describe how history was made.

🏛️ Build Construir V3: Built
🧭 Discover Descubrir V3: Discovered
💡 Invent Inventar V3: Invented
📜 Found Fundar (Establecer) V3: Founded

2. Grammar: Past Simple Passive

To talk about when things were created in the past, we use the Passive Voice. Instead of using am/is/are, we use the past tense: was (singular) or were (plural).

Singular (WAS)
One Object + WAS + V3
"The school was built in 1990."
"America was discovered in 1492."
Plural (WERE)
Many Objects + WERE + V3
"The pyramids were built in Egypt."
"The tests were graded yesterday."
🚨 THE "FOUND" VS "FOUNDED" TRAP

This is a legendary grammar mistake! The verb Find (Encontrar) is irregular: its past is Found. But the verb Found (Fundar una ciudad o club) is regular! Its past is Founded. When talking about history, you almost always need "Founded"!

❌ Sucre was found in 1538. (Sucre fue encontrada)
✅ Sucre was FOUNDED in 1538. (Sucre fue fundada)

"As an educator, I love teaching History of Bolivia to my students from the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, and 4th grades at Colegio Fielding. I always tell them to look at our beautiful city. Sucre was founded in 1538 by Pedro Anzúrez. Many historic buildings were built centuries ago."

"But history is not just about old cities; it is about organizations too! Besides teaching, I work as a graphic designer. Recently, I was designing branding materials for the Mutual de Exjugadores de Fútbol Sucre. I learned that the club was founded in 1996!"

"Of course, history happens at home too. Yesterday, a tragedy occurred. My new HTML codes were deleted by Perlita the cat! And my CrossFit shoes, which were bought by Lau last year, were used as her new bed. Some history is better forgotten!"
