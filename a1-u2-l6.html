Always and Never

Objective: Talk about how often you do things using Adverbs of Frequency.

1. Vocabulary: Frequency

These words answer the question "How often?" (¿Con qué frecuencia?).

100% Always Siempre
80% Usually Usualmente / Normalmente
50% Sometimes A veces
0% Never Nunca

2. Grammar: Where do I put the word?

In English, the position of the adverb is very strict. It must go BEFORE the main verb (action).

The Standard Rule
Subject + Adverb + Verb

Put the frequency word between the person and the action.

"I always wake up early." (Yo siempre despierto temprano).
"He never eats pizza." (Él nunca come pizza).
🚨 The "To Be" Exception

The verb "To Be" (am / is / are) is special. It does not follow the normal rules. If you use the verb "To Be", the adverb must go AFTER it.

Formula: Subject + AM/IS/ARE + Adverb

❌ She always is happy.
✅ She IS always happy.

Reading Context: Good and Bad Habits

"I am a very disciplined person. I always wake up at 5:30 AM to run. I usually go to the CrossFit gym on Mondays and Wednesdays. I sometimes eat a hamburger on the weekend, but I never eat sugar before a class."

"My students at Colegio Fielding are good students. They usually do their homework. They are always happy in my Philosophy class."

"But Perlita is different. She is never active. She never runs. She sometimes plays with a toy, but she usually sleeps. She is always tired!"
