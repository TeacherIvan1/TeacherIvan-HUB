What time is it?

Objective: Learn to tell the time and describe your daily schedule.

1. Vocabulary: The Clock

Learn the key words to read the clock in English.

🕛 O'clock En punto (Ej: 12:00)
🕧 Half past Y media (Ej: 12:30)
🕒 Quarter past Y cuarto (Ej: 3:15)
🕤 Quarter to Menos cuarto (Ej: 9:45)

2. Grammar: Telling the Time

When someone asks you "What time is it?", you always answer with "It is...".

07:00
It is seven o'clock.
07:15
It is quarter past seven.
07:30
It is half past seven.
07:45
It is quarter to eight.
🚨 The Magic Preposition: "AT"

In Spanish, we say "Yo como a las 8:00". In English, NEVER translate "a las". For times on the clock, we always use the preposition AT.

❌ I eat breakfast to the 8:00.
✅ I eat breakfast AT 8:00.

Reading Context: Thursday Morning Routine

"Today is Thursday in Sucre. What time is it? It is half past five in the morning (5:30 AM). My alarm rings. I wake up early because I go for a running session!"

"After my run, I am hungry. I eat breakfast at quarter past seven (7:15 AM). I eat eggs, oats, and avocado. It is healthy food. Then, at eight o'clock (8:00 AM), I arrive at Colegio Fielding to teach English to my students."

"And what about Perlita the cat? It is nine o'clock (9:00 AM) and she is still in bed. She wakes up at half past eleven (11:30 AM). She is very lazy!"
