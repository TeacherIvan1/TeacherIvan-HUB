2. Grammar: The First Conditional

We use the First Conditional to talk about a real, possible situation in the future. It has two parts: the Condition (If) and the Result (Will).

The Condition
IF + Present Simple

This is the action that needs to happen.

"If you study hard..."
"If she logs in every day..."
The Result
WILL / WON'T + Verb

This is what happens in the future as a consequence.

"...you will pass the exam."
"...she won't fail the class."
🚨 THE DOUBLE "WILL" TRAP

Because we are talking about the future, many students put "will" in both parts of the sentence. In English, the "IF" part NEVER takes "will".

❌ If it will rain, I will stay home.
✅ If it RAINS (Present Simple), I will stay home.
