We aren't going to quit

Objective: Express determination and future intentions using the negative form of "Be going to".

1. Vocabulary: Determination & Outcomes

Learn these verbs to express what you intend to achieve or avoid in your goals.

🛑 Quit Rendirse / Abandonar
🏳️ Give up Darse por vencido
❌ Fail Reprobar / Fracasar
🏆 Win Ganar

2. Grammar: Negative "Be Going To"

To say that you do not intend to do something in the future, simply add NOT after the verb To Be (am / is / are). We usually use short contractions (*isn't / aren't*).

Full Form
Subject + am / is / are + NOT + going to + Verb
"I am NOT going to quit."
"He is NOT going to fail."
"We are NOT going to give up."
Contracted Form (Most Natural)
isn't / aren't + going to + Verb

*Note: "I am not" has no "amn't" contraction. Only write "I'm not".

"I'm not going to quit."
"He isn't going to fail."
"We aren't going to give up."
🚨 THE "NO GOING TO" TRAP

In Spanish, you negate the sentence by just saying "no" before the verb ("No voy a rendirme"). In English, you cannot say "I no going to" or "We not going to". The verb To Be is mandatory!

❌ We no going to quit the competition.
❌ We not going to quit the competition.
✅ We AREN'T going to quit the competition.

Reading Context: The Toughest Workout

"Yesterday, the workout at the CrossFit box in Sucre was incredibly hard. Lau and I were lifting heavy barbells, and our muscles were burning. At one point, I looked at her and said: 'This is exhausting!' She shouted back: 'Yes, it is, but we aren't going to quit! We aren't going to give up today! We want to win!'"

"That mental discipline helps me in my job too. At Colegio Fielding, my students work hard because they know the rules. A student told me today: 'Teacher Ivan, I am studying every day. I isn't going to fail your History class!' (Well, he needs to improve his grammar, but his intention is perfect!)."

"At home, our cat El Michi has a very different mindset. He isn't going to train CrossFit, and he isn't going to move from the sofa. He is just going to rest all night!"
