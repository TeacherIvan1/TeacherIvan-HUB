CrossFit Equipment

Objective: Compare equipment and describe extremes using Comparatives and Superlatives.

1. Vocabulary: In the Box

Learn these words to talk about the equipment we use in a CrossFit box.

🏋️‍♂️ Barbell Barra (de pesas)
🪨 Kettlebell Pesa rusa
➰ Jump rope Cuerda de saltar
💨 Chalk Magnesio (Polvo para manos)

2. Grammar: Comparatives & Superlatives

When you want to compare two objects, or talk about the extreme of a group, we change the adjective depending on its size (short or long).

Comparatives (Comparing 2 things)
Short: Adj + ER + than
Long: More + Adj + than

Use than (que) to connect the two objects.

"The barbell is heavier than the kettlebell."
"CrossFit is more difficult than running."
Superlatives (The extreme)
Short: The + Adj + EST
Long: The most + Adj

Use the (el/la) because it is unique in the group.

"El Michi is the laziest cat."
"This is the most expensive equipment."
🚨 THE "MORE FAST" TRAP

Because in Spanish we say "Más rápido" or "Más pesado", students often try to say "More fast" or "More heavy". In English, if the adjective is short (1 syllable, or 2 ending in 'y'), you MUST add -ER. You cannot use "More".

❌ Lau is more fast than me.
✅ Lau is FASTER than me.

Reading Context: Training for Seishin

"When Lau and I train for a CrossFit competition like Seishin, we use a lot of equipment in the box. For me, the barbell is heavier than the kettlebell, but the Clean & Jerk is the most difficult movement! Lau is faster than me with the jump rope."

"We always use chalk for our hands before lifting. Chalk is the best way to grab the bar safely during an AMRAP (As Many Rounds As Possible). Good running shoes are more important than normal shoes for sprinting."

"At home, my cat El Michi doesn't lift weights. He is the laziest cat in Sucre. For him, jumping on the sofa is more tiring than an intense AMRAP! We definitely have different energy levels."
