Are you going to travel?

Objective: Ask questions and give short answers about future plans using "Be going to".

1. Vocabulary: Travel Planning

Learn these verbs to describe your actions before and during a trip.

🧳 Pack a suitcase Hacer la maleta
🏨 Book a hotel Reservar un hotel
✈️ Fly Volar / Ir en avión
🗺️ Travel Viajar

2. Grammar: Future Questions & Short Answers

To ask about a plan in English, we change the order of the sentence. We put the verb To Be (Am/Is/Are) at the very beginning.

The Question Formula
Am / Is / Are + Subject + going to + Verb?
"Are you going to travel?"
"Is she going to book the hotel?"
Short Answers

Don't just say "Yes" or "No". Use the verb To Be to answer cleanly.

➔ "Yes, I am. / No, I'm not."
➔ "Yes, she is. / No, she isn't."
➔ "Yes, we are. / No, we aren't."
🚨 THE INVERSION TRAP

In Spanish, you change a sentence into a question using just your voice intonación ("Tú vas a viajar?"). In English, if you don't swap the order of the first two words, it is incorrect!

❌ You are going to book the room?
✅ ARE YOU going to book the room?

Reading Context: Planning the Next CrossFit Trip

"Lau and I are organizing our calendar for next month. Yesterday, she looked at me and asked: 'Are we going to travel to Santa Cruz for the upcoming CrossFit competition?' I smiled and answered: 'Yes, we are!'"

"Then, I had a question for her: 'Are you going to pack all the skipping ropes and chalk in the suitcase?' She laughed and said: 'Yes, I am. I already booked the hotel!'"

"Finally, Lau asked about our pet: 'Is El Michi going to fly with us?' I replied: 'No, he isn't. He absolutely hates flying! He is going to stay in Sucre with my friend Nuevo Boris. He is going to sleep on the sofa all weekend!'"
