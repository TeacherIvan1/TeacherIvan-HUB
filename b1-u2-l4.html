He had already finished

Objective: Talk about "the past of the past" using the Past Perfect and Time Markers.

1. Vocabulary: Time Markers

These words are essential to explain the order of events in the past.

✅ Already Ya (Ocurrió antes de lo esperado)
⚡ Just Justo ahora / Acabar de...
⏱️ By the time Para cuando...
🔄 Before Antes de

2. Grammar: The Past Perfect

Imagine two things happened yesterday. To show which action happened FIRST (the oldest action), we use the Past Perfect. For the newer action, we use the Past Simple.

Action 1 (Oldest) ──────➔ Action 2 (Newer) ──────➔ NOW
PAST PERFECT             PAST SIMPLE              PRESENT
The Formula
Subject + HAD + Past Participle (V3)

You must use HAD for all subjects (I, you, he, she, we, they) and the 3rd column of the verb.

"When I arrived, she had already left." (Cuando llegué, ella ya se había ido).
"By the time we ate, he had finished." (Para cuando comimos, él había terminado).
🚨 THE "HAVE" VS "HAD" TRAP

Many students translate "había" as "have" or "has". That is Present Perfect! If you are telling a story about yesterday, you MUST use HAD.

❌ When I arrived, the class has started.
✅ When I arrived, the class HAD started.

Reading Context: Always Late!

"Yesterday was a terrible day. First, my alarm didn't ring. By the time I arrived at Colegio Fielding, the Philosophy class was completely quiet. The students had already opened their books and started reading without me!"

"After work, I was very hungry. I went home to eat lunch with my girlfriend. But when I walked into the kitchen, she was washing the dishes. She had already eaten all the food! She said she was too hungry after CrossFit."

"I was sad, so I looked for Perlita the cat to pet her. But before I touched her, she ran away. She had just woken up from a 5-hour nap and she wanted her tuna. I was totally alone!"
