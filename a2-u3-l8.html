2. Grammar: Infinitive of Purpose

When someone asks you "Why?" (¿Por qué?), you need to explain your purpose. To do this in English, we simply use the word TO + the action verb.

The Formula
Main Action + TO + Base Verb
"I am going to study to pass the exam."
"She will travel to compete in Santa Cruz."
"He practices every day to achieve his goals."
🚨 THE "FOR" TRAP

In Spanish, we say "Estudio para aprender". Because "para" usually translates to "for", many students say "I study for learn" or "for to learn". This is completely wrong in English!

❌ I read books for understand English.
❌ I read books for to understand English.
✅ I read books TO UNDERSTAND English.
