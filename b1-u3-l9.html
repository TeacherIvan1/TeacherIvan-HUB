"Creating the 'Teacher Ivan HUB' was a massive challenge. If I hadn't backed up my HTML code on Google Drive last month, I would have lost everything when my computer crashed! That is the laptop which I use for all my classes at Colegio Fielding.

Now, the platform is running perfectly. Provided that my students log in every day, they will get the full 45 points in the 'Saber' dimension. However, unless they track their progress, they will not succeed in their final exams.

Sometimes I dream about the future. If I had a million dollars, I would build a Virtual Reality school for History and English. I can already imagine it: in the year 2040, I will be teaching students all over Bolivia as a hologram, from the exact same gaming chair where Perlita the cat is sleeping right now. My girlfriend Lau is the person who always supports my crazy ideas!"

2. B1 Unit 3 Cheatsheet

The Conditionals
• 1st (Real Future): If + Present, Will.
• 2nd (Imaginary): If + Past Simple, Would.
• 3rd (Regrets): If + Had V3, Would have V3.
Advanced Linking
• Unless: If not. (Never use negatives after it).
• Provided that: Formal version of 'As long as'.
• Future Continuous: Will be + V-ing (Action in progress).
Relative Clauses
• Who: For people (Lau is the person who...).
• Which/That: For objects (The laptop that...).
• Where: For places (The chair where...).
