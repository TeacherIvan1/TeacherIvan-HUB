Reviewing Tech Gadgets

Objective: Describe objects, people, and places clearly using Relative Clauses.

To write a good tech review, you need to know the names of the devices.

2. Grammar: Relative Clauses

Instead of making two short sentences ("I have a laptop. It is fast."), we use a Relative Pronoun to connect them ("I have a laptop which is fast."). You must choose the right word depending on what you are describing.

WHO (People)
Person + WHO + Action

Only use Who when talking about a human being.

"He is the teacher who built the app."
WHICH / THAT (Things)
Object + WHICH/THAT + Action

Use Which or That for animals, gadgets, or objects.

"This is the smartwatch which tracks my steps."
WHERE (Places)
Place + WHERE + Subject + Action

Use Where to describe a location or a physical space.

"This is the room where I record my videos."
🚨 THE "WHAT" TRAP

In Spanish, we say "El reloj *que* compré". Many students translate "que" as "what". In English, "what" is a question word, NOT a connector for objects! Always use That or Which.

❌ The headphones what I bought are amazing.
✅ The headphones THAT / WHICH I bought are amazing.

Reading Context: Teacher Ivan's Tech Review

"As an educator and a gamer, I rely on technology every day. Let me review the gadgets which I use the most. First, my noise-canceling headphones. These are the headphones that I use to edit my class videos on CapCut Pro and to play Warcraft III: Reforged. I love them!"

"Next is my smartwatch. This is the device which tracks my 5:30 AM running sessions and my intense CrossFit workouts. I bought it in a sports store in Sucre where they sell professional gear."

"Of course, the most important gadget is the laptop that I use to code the 'Teacher Ivan HUB' for Colegio Fielding. I also have a very comfortable gaming chair. However, the chair is the place where Perlita the cat loves to sleep! She is the only pet who thinks my gaming setup is her personal bed."
