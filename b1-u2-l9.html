1. Reading: The App Emergency

Read Teacher Ivan's story. Pay close attention to how the tenses and connectors link the events together.

"Last month, my girlfriend and I set off for a CrossFit competition in Santa Cruz. While we were travelling on the bus, my laptop suddenly died. I couldn't believe it! I had forgotten to charge it the night before.

I was extremely worried because my new application, 'Teacher Ivan - Lifestyle', had an automated update scheduled for that exact time. Just as we arrived at the terminal, my phone rang. It was the principal from Colegio Fielding. As soon as I answered, he told me that the school's server had crashed!

I needed Wi-Fi immediately, so I rushed to an internet cafe. Meanwhile, Perlita was back in Sucre. She was happily sleeping on the sofa, completely unaware of the crisis. I worked frantically on the HTML code. Eventually, the server restarted. We didn't win the CrossFit competition, but saving the app was a massive victory."

2. B1 Narrative Cheatsheet

Narrative Tenses
• Past Continuous: The long action in progress (was travelling).
• Past Simple: The short, sudden interruption (the phone rang).
• Past Perfect: The 'past of the past' (I had forgotten). Always use HAD + V3.
Advanced Connectors
• While / When: While + Continuous. When + Simple.
• So / Because: Reason (because) vs. Result (so).
• As soon as / Meanwhile: Immediate action vs. Parallel action.
Travel & Prepositions
• IN vs ON: In a car/taxi (can't walk). On a bus/plane (can walk).
• Eventually: Means 'Finally'. It does NOT mean 'De vez en cuando'.
• Phrasal Verbs: Set off (start), break down (stop working).
