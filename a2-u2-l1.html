Expressing Preferences

When we want to talk about hobbies or activities we like (or don't like), we use a verb of preference followed by another verb ending in -ING.

The Scale of Preference

  • 😍 love + ING (I love running)
  • 😊 like / enjoy + ING (She enjoys swimming)
  • 😐 don't mind + ING (We don't mind walking)
  • 😠 don't like + ING (He doesn't like losing)
  • 😡 hate + ING (They hate waiting)
⚠️ Spelling Rules for -ING:
1. End in 'e' ➔ drop 'e', add -ing (dance ➔ dancing)
2. CVC (Consonant-Vowel-Consonant) ➔ double the last consonant (run ➔ running)
