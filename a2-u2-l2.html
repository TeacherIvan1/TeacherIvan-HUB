How often do you train?

Objective: Talk about routines using Adverbs of Frequency (always, usually, never).

1. Vocabulary: Frequency

These words tell us "how many times" you do an activity.

💯 Always Siempre (100%)
👍 Usually / Often Usualmente / A menudo (80%)
🤷‍♂️ Sometimes A veces (50%)
🚫 Never Nunca (0%)

2. Grammar: Where do we put the Adverb?

In English, you cannot put the frequency word anywhere you want. It depends on the verb you are using.

Rule 1: Normal Verbs
Subject + Adverb + Verb

The adverb goes BEFORE the action.

"I always run in the morning."
"She usually trains CrossFit."
Rule 2: Verb To Be
Subject + To Be + Adverb

The adverb goes AFTER am, is, or are.

"I am never late."
"The gym is usually full."
🚨 THE DOUBLE NEGATIVE TRAP

The word "Never" is already negative. You cannot use "don't" or "doesn't" with "Never".

❌ I don't never skip training.
✅ I never skip training.

Reading Context: My Training Schedule

"As an athlete and a teacher, I have a very busy schedule in Sucre. People often ask me: 'Teacher Ivan, how often do you train?'"

"I always run at 5:30 AM. It is my daily habit. After that, I usually teach my English and History classes at Colegio Fielding. In the evenings, Lau and I sometimes go to the CrossFit box. We train together four times a week to prepare for competitions."

"I never eat bad food because nutrition is important for weightlifting. However, my cat El Michi never trains. He is always tired and usually sleeps on the sofa all day. He is rarely active!"
