Global Warming

Objective: Discuss ongoing environmental issues using the Present Continuous Passive.

1. Vocabulary: The Environment

Learn these regular verbs to discuss actions affecting our planet right now.

🏭 Pollute Contaminar V3: Polluted
🔥 Destroy Destruir V3: Destroyed
🛡️ Protect Proteger V3: Protected
♻️ Recycle Reciclar V3: Recycled

2. Grammar: Present Continuous Passive

When an action is happening right now to an object, we use the Present Continuous Passive. We must add the word BEING to show that the action is currently in progress.

Singular (Is being)
One Object + IS BEING + V3
"The planet is being polluted." (El planeta está siendo contaminado).
"A new park is being protected."
Plural (Are being)
Many Objects + ARE BEING + V3
"Forests are being destroyed." (Los bosques están siendo destruidos).
"Plastic bottles are being recycled."
🚨 THE ACTIVE OBJECT TRAP

If you forget the word "BEING" and just use the verb ending in "-ing", you are saying the object is doing the action! Trees cannot cut things, and oceans cannot pollute things.

❌ The trees are cutting down. (¡Los árboles están talando cosas!)
✅ The trees are BEING CUT down. (Los árboles están siendo talados).

Reading Context: The Heat in Sucre

"Global warming is a reality. Even here in Sucre, the weather is being affected. When I do CrossFit at 6:00 AM, the mornings are much hotter than before. In my history classes at Colegio Fielding, we discuss how the environment is being destroyed by human actions."

"But positive things are being done too! In our home, plastic is being reduced. For example, reusable bags are being used by Lau when she goes to the market, and old clothes are being recycled instead of thrown away."

"Even our cat, El Michi (Perlita), helps the planet! Every time an Amazon delivery arrives, the cardboard boxes are being recycled into new beds for him. He sleeps inside them all day. So yes, nature is being protected... by a very lazy cat!"
