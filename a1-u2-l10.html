Project: My Perfect Day

Objective: Write a paragraph describing your ideal day using routines, time, and future plans.

1. Your Writing Mission 🚀

It is time to put everything together! Write a short text about your perfect weekend day. Tell me what time you wake up, what you usually do, and what your plans are for the afternoon.

✍️ The Prompt

Write a paragraph about your perfect Saturday. You must include:

  • The time you wake up (using AT).
  • Two things you do in the morning or in the afternoon.
  • One Adverb of Frequency (always, usually, sometimes, never).
  • One future plan (I am going to...).

Write at least 50 words.

Teacher Ivan's Example:
"On Saturday, I always wake up at 7:00 AM. In the morning, I go to the gym and I train CrossFit with my girlfriend. I never work at Colegio Fielding on the weekend! In the afternoon, I am going to write HTML code for my new app. At night, Perlita and I relax."
