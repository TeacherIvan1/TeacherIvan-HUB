I don't mind lifting

Objective: Express neutral preferences and strong dislikes using verbs + ING.

1. Vocabulary: Fitness Actions

Learn these action verbs to talk about working out in the gym.

🏋️‍♂️ Lift Levantar (pesas) Action: Lifting
💧 Sweat Sudar Action: Sweating
🧘‍♂️ Stretch Estirar Action: Stretching
⏱️ Rest Descansar Action: Resting

2. Grammar: Don't Mind vs. Can't Stand

We know how to say we "love" or "hate" things. But what if our feelings are neutral? Or what if we want a stronger word for "hate"?

I don't mind (Neutral)
Subject + don't/doesn't mind + Verb-ING

It means: "It's okay for me. It is not a problem." (No me molesta / No me importa hacerlo).

"I don't mind sweating at the gym."
"He doesn't mind running in the rain."
I can't stand (Strong Dislike)
Subject + can't stand + Verb-ING

It means: "I hate it very much. I cannot tolerate it." (No lo soporto).

"She can't stand waiting."
🚨 THE INFINITIVE TRAP

Just like with "love" and "enjoy" from Lesson 1, you MUST add -ING after "don't mind" and "can't stand". Do not use "to" + verb.

❌ I don't mind to lift heavy weights.
✅ I don't mind lifting heavy weights.

Reading Context: The CrossFit Mindset

"CrossFit is not for everybody, but I really enjoy it. When I train in Sucre, I don't mind lifting heavy weights. Doing the Clean & Jerk is my favorite part! I don't mind sweating because it means I am working hard."

"Lau also trains with me. She doesn't mind stretching before the workout. However, she can't stand resting too long between sets. She wants to keep moving!"

"When we finish, we go back home. El Michi looks at us from the sofa. He can't stand loud noises, but he doesn't mind watching us prepare our food. For him, sleeping is the ultimate workout!"
