The 5:30 AM Run

Objective: Talk about your morning routine using Present Simple verbs (I, We).

1. Vocabulary: Morning Routine

Learn the essential actions to start your day.

🌅 Wake up Despertarse
🏃‍♂️ Go running Ir a correr
🚿 Take a shower Bañarse / Tomar una ducha
👕 Get dressed Vestirse

2. Grammar: Present Simple (I / We)

We use the Present Simple to talk about habits and daily routines. For the subjects I, You, We, and They, the verb stays in its normal, base form.

Affirmative (+)
Subject + Verb

Just put the person and the action together.

"I wake up early."
"We go running."
Negative (-)
Subject + don't + Verb

To say "no", we use the magic word don't (do not).

"I don't sleep late."
"We don't eat pizza."
🚨 The "I AM" Trap

Never use "am" with these routine verbs! "I am" means "Yo soy" or "Yo estoy". If you say "I am wake up", you are saying "Yo soy despertar".

❌ I am take a shower at 6:00.
✅ I take a shower at 6:00.

Reading Context: The Thursday Run

"Every Thursday, I have private English classes. Because of this, my morning routine is very active! My girlfriend and I wake up at 5:30 AM. We don't sleep late."

"We go running together. It is very cold in Sucre in the mornings, but we don't stop. After the run, we go back home. I take a shower at 6:30 AM and I get dressed for work."

"Then, I eat breakfast. I don't eat sugar. I like healthy food. Finally, I go to Colegio Fielding to teach."
