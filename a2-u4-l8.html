Good, Better, Best

Objective: Master the irregular adjectives that don't follow the normal rules.

1. Vocabulary: The Irregulars

Learn these three special words. They change completely when comparing!

👍 Good Bueno
👎 Bad Malo
📍 Far Lejos
⭐ Well Bien (Salud/Acción)

2. Grammar: The Irregular Table

The rules of "-er" and "-est" DO NOT WORK for these adjectives. You must memorize their new forms for Comparatives (2 things) and Superlatives (the number 1).

The "Good" Family
Good ➔ Better ➔ The best
"This book is better than that one."
"He is the best student."
The "Bad" Family
Bad ➔ Worse ➔ The worst
"The weather is worse than yesterday."
"This is the worst movie ever."
The "Far" Family (Distance)
Far ➔ Further ➔ The furthest
"La Paz is further than Potosí from Sucre."
🚨 THE "GOODER" TRAP

Because you learned to say "faster" and "taller", your brain wants to say "gooder" and "badder". These words DO NOT EXIST in English! Erase them from your mind.

❌ My English is gooder than before.
❌ He is the baddest player.
✅ My English is BETTER. / He is the WORST player.
