2. A1 Grammar Cheatsheet

Time & Days
• AT: For clock times (at 8:00) and night (at night).
• ON: For days of the week (on Monday).
• IN: For parts of the day (in the morning).
Present Simple
• I / You / We / They: I play, I don't play, Do you play?
• He / She / It: She plays, She doesn't play, Does she play?
• Trap: Never use two "S"s! (Does she play?)
Frequency & Future
• Adverbs: Go BEFORE the verb (I always eat), but AFTER 'To Be' (She is always happy).
• Future Plans: Use am/is/are + going to + verb (I am going to study).
