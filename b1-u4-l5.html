2. Grammar: The Agent with "BY"

In the Passive Voice, the person or thing that does the action is called the Agent. If we want to mention the Agent because it is important or interesting, we use the word BY at the end of the sentence.

Important Agent (Use BY)

Use it when the creator is famous, specific, or surprising.

"Harry Potter was written by J.K. Rowling."
"The app was designed by Teacher Ivan."
Obvious/Unknown Agent (Omit it)

Do NOT use "by" if the agent is obvious or unknown (like "by people" or "by someone").

"English is spoken in Australia."
(Not: by people)
"My car was stolen!"
(Not: by someone)
🚨 THE "FOR" VS "BY" TRAP

In Spanish, we translate both "por" and "para" in confusing ways. Remember: BY means who did it. FOR means who received it.

❌ The poem was written for Shakespeare. (Esto significa que alguien le escribió un poema a él).
✅ The poem was written BY Shakespeare. (Él es el autor).
