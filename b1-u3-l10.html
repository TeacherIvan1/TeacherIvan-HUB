Project: My App Idea

Objective: Write a 100-word pitch for a new application using advanced B1 grammar.

1. Your Tech Pitch 🚀

Imagine you are a software developer. You must pitch (present) a new app idea to an investor. Describe what the app does, who it is for, and what would happen if it becomes successful.

✍️ The Prompt

Write a text explaining your app. You must include:

  • Relative Clauses: Use Who, Which, or Where to describe the app and the users.
  • 1st Conditional / Unless: Explain the rules of the app (If users log in... / Unless they pay...).
  • 2nd Conditional: Imagine what you would do with the app (If I had more time, I would...).
  • 3rd Conditional: Share a past development story (If I hadn't studied HTML, I wouldn't have built it...).

Write at least 100 words.

Teacher Ivan's Example: The Lifestyle Tracker

"If I hadn't learned HTML and CSS, I would never have built 'Teacher Ivan - Lifestyle'. This is an app which helps students from Colegio Fielding track their habits and grades.

The app is for people who want to improve their routines. Provided that my students use it every day, they will pass the class easily. However, unless they complete the 40 points in the 'Hacer' dimension, the app will send an alert.

If I had unlimited money, I would add a CrossFit tracker for my girlfriend Lau and me. Next year, I will be launching a special feature for pets, specifically for the sofa where Perlita sleeps all day!"
