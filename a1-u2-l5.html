2. Grammar: Questions & Short Answers

To ask a question in English, you CANNOT just change your voice. You must put a magic word at the beginning of the sentence: DO or DOES.

DO (I / You / We / They)
DO + Subject + Verb + ?
"Do you train today?"
✅ Yes, I do.
❌ No, I don't.
DOES (He / She / It)
DOES + Subject + Verb + ?
"Does she train today?"
✅ Yes, she does.
❌ No, she doesn't.
🚨 The "Invisible DO" Trap

In Spanish, we say: "¿Tú entrenas?". In English, if you say "You train?", people will not understand it is a question. You MUST start with DO or DOES.

Also, remember the "S" rule: In questions with DOES, the verb loses the "S"!
❌ Does he trains?
✅ Does he TRAIN?

Reading Context: The Gym Interview

Interviewer: Hello Ivan! Do you train today?

Ivan: Yes, I do. I love CrossFit.

Interviewer: And your girlfriend? Does she work out with you?

Ivan: Yes, she does. We go to the gym together early in the morning.

Interviewer: That is great! I have one more question. Does Perlita go to the gym?

Ivan: Haha! No, she doesn't. Perlita is a cat. She doesn't train. She rests on the sofa all day!
