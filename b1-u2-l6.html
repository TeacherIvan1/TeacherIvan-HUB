2. Grammar: Time Prepositions in History

When we talk about history, the accuracy of dates is crucial. You must choose the right preposition depending on how exact the time is.

IN (Years, Months, Centuries)
IN + 1809 / July / The 19th Century

Use IN for general, broad periods of time.

"The revolution started in 1809."
ON (Specific Dates)
ON + July 16 / Monday / July 16, 1809

Use ON if the date includes the specific day.

"They signed the document on July 16, 1809."
DURING (Throughout a period)
DURING + Noun (the war, the year, the meeting)

Use DURING to say that something happened within a specific block of time.

"People suffered a lot during the Spanish rule."
🚨 THE DATE TRAP

In Spanish, we say "En el 16 de julio". But in English, if there is a DAY (16) attached to the month, it immediately changes from IN to ON.

❌ The uprising happened IN July 16.
✅ The uprising happened ON July 16.
✅ The uprising happened IN July.
